Dental examination

A full look at teeth, gums and jaw, with photographs so you can see what we see.

DisciplineDentistryMinutes40

Most people book an examination because something twinged once and then stopped. That is a good reason to come.

We check every tooth surface, measure the gum pockets, look at the soft tissue of the mouth and feel the jaw joint while you open and close. X-rays are taken only when there is a question they can answer, and we will tell you what the question is.

At the end you get photographs on the screen, a written plan in order of urgency, and a clear line between what needs doing now, what can be watched, and what is cosmetic. If nothing needs doing, we say so and book you back in six or twelve months.

If you are nervous

Tell reception when you book. We will put you in the first slot of the day, keep the door open, and stop whenever you raise a hand. Nobody is talked into treatment in the chair.
