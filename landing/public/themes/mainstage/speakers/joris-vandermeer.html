Joris Vandermeer

Chef, Antwerp

Joris cooks over open fire and serves in the dark — or close to it. His dinners run at candle level, on the theory that when you can barely see the plate, you taste in a different order. The menus are short, seasonal and cooked within sight of the table.

He grew up in his family's harbor-side canteen and still builds menus around what a working port eats: smoked fish, root vegetables, bread that keeps. The Glasshouse dinner at Lumen is his first collaboration with a lighting designer instead of an electrician.
