Noor El-Amin

Filmmaker, Marseille

Noor makes documentaries shot almost entirely at night, on the streets and in the workplaces most films skip: container terminals, bakeries at 3 a.m., toll booths. She works with available light and a two-person crew, and her films are as much about sound as image.

At Lumen she screens her harbor triptych with the score performed live, and stays for a conversation about filming people whose working day ends when everyone else's begins.
