Two Roofs

A house split in half so an ageing parent and a young family can live one wall apart.

Nyholt · 2025 · Completed

Two households, one plot, and a relationship that needed a door between it rather than an open plan. We drew two houses of unequal size sharing a masonry spine, each with its own entrance, its own heating and its own roof.

The smaller half is on one level, with a bathroom sized for a wheelchair from the start rather than adapted later. The larger half has three bedrooms upstairs and a kitchen that opens onto the shared yard, which is the only place the two houses are obliged to meet.

The spine wall is 300mm of solid brick. It carries both roofs, holds the flues, and means neither household can hear the other's television.
