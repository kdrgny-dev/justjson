After you move in

A manual for the building, and a visit at one year to fix what has moved.

You get a folder that explains the house: where the valves are, how the ventilation runs, which paint we used in which room, and who made the windows. It is written to be read by somebody who is not you, ten years from now.

We come back at one year. Timber shrinks, plaster cracks along its first winter, doors that closed perfectly in August need easing by February. That visit is included and we fix what we find. After that we are still the people to call, and we still have the drawings.
