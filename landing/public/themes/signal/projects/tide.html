Tide

Summary
A tracker for cold-water swimmers: temperature, duration, and how it felt afterwards.

Body

Swimmers were logging sessions in notes apps and spreadsheets: water temperature, time in, how the rest of the day went. Tide keeps that shape — three fields and a note — because the people who do this every morning did not want a fitness app.

Entry takes under ten seconds with cold hands: large targets, no keyboard for the common case, and nothing that requires unlocking a phone with wet fingers twice.

The interesting screen turned out not to be the log but the year: 340 swims drawn as one column per day, colour by temperature. It is the only part people share.
